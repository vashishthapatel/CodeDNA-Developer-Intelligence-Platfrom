Inside Your DNA Score

Every repository you connect feeds five weighted signals. Together they resolve into a single number out of 100 — and a ranked list of what to learn next.

100 points

Five weighted signals, one composite score

  • Code volume

    25%

    Commits, additions and deletions across every repository you own or contribute to.

  • Language breadth

    20%

    How many ecosystems you actually ship in — and how deep each one runs.

  • Commit consistency

    20%

    Rhythm beats bursts: a steady streak scores above one heroic weekend.

  • Collaboration

    20%

    Pull requests, reviews and issues — the work that happens with other people.

  • Code quality

    15%

    Tests, documentation and structure, read straight from the repositories themselves.

Requires repo · read:user · read:org

Three scopes are enough: repo to read private repositories, read:user for your profile, and read:org for organisation repositories. Grant less and the private half stays empty.

The token is kept in this browser's localStorage so the dashboard survives a reload. Anything with access to this browser profile can read it — revoke it from GitHub's settings when you are done.
