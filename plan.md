# CodeDNA UI polish plan

## Objective
Refine the landing page and dashboard to feel cohesive, premium, and high-end while preserving app logic and backend behavior.

## Current status
- The previous ambient 3D and glass-heavy styling has been removed.
- The app and landing pages now use a cleaner, darker CodeDNA palette with more editorial typography and lower visual noise.
- The dashboard and landing shell have been simplified to match the premium minimal reference without affecting app logic.

## Next steps
1. Confirm the updated theme renders consistently across the dashboard and landing routes.
2. Validate the app still builds and serves locally after the CSS-only redesign.
3. Keep any future refinements focused on spacing and typography rather than motion-heavy effects.
